Instance Settings

To start you should set your allocated RAM to 4GB

Under "JVM Arguments" change 2G to 4G, but make sure, that it fits in your maximum RAM shown in Task-Manager under RAM in the top right corner

In-Game Setting

On most Seasions we were running following Settings

  • Keep Inventory
  • Easy Mode
  • PvP off
