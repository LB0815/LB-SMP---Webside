What are the rules

  • No Griefing
  • No Hacking
  • No Mocking
  • No Harassing
  • No X-Ray

The consequences

Most consequences will be either an Strike, Kick or 1 day ban

But in some cases you will face harder consequences.

  • Hacking → Permanent Ban
  • X-Ray → 7 day Ban
  • Griefing → Adventure Mode

How to get unbaned?

In most cases just wait out your time period, but if the ban is permanent, then you should politely ask the Admins for an unban.
